Mana Mei is a professional dancer and an International Leader in the Art of Feminine Embodiment.

She is a mother of 2 incredible children, a devoted creative and nature-lover.

She is the founder of Liberation Through Movement- a global somatic healing and personal transformation modality for women based on embodied movement and somatic dance.

Mana Movement® was birthed in 2019 through a series of synchronistic events that led Mana Mei to begin the inspired creation of the practice.

Coming from a background of classical dance technique and free dance exploration, Mana dove into the intersection of her entire life’s education of dance, spirituality, and personal development to curate “the perfect practice”.

Mana Mei has been obsessed with dance her entire life.

She began her life-long journey with movement at just 3 years old. Mana studied classical ballet, modern and contemporary dance, somatic dance, improvisation, as well as ecstatic dance, 5 rhythms, and intuitive free movement.

Mana has been devoted to her personal development for the last 20 years, and taking her down many pathways of self exploration including vipassana meditation, somatic breathwork, tantra, Feminine Embodiment & women’s work, plant medicines and psychedelics, martial arts & eastern spirituality, and somatic therapy.

Mana Mei has dedicated her life to establishing a deep unwavering sense of inner safety and freedom for herself, and has extended this devotion to helping other women do the same through her work as a women’s embodiment mentor and facilitator.

Mana Mei currently resides in Nevada City, California.
