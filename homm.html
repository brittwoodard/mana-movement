More on Mana Movement

To realign with pulse of nature is to open every aspect of your life to clarity and flow.

Through activating the Feminine Pathways of movement- circle, spirals and waves- through your physical and energetic body, you can enter a state of coherence with the Universal energy, increasing vitality, innate wisdom, love and creativity.

Mana Movement is a holistic embodied movement practice that realigns your body, mind, and spirit with the pulse of nature— clearing stagnant energy, awakening life force, and restoring your connection to your innate source of power.

Through guided movement and breath sequences focused on increasing whole body fluidity through the Feminine Pathways and feeling body presence, Mana Movement will awaken deep sense of wholeness, peace, and coherence with the flow of life as it moves within and without.

Founded on foundational techniques of somatic movement, embodied dance, mindfulness meditation, and Breathwork, Mana Movement is a powerful, yet accessible Healing Modality to cultivate unwavering safety and freedom within the body through a gentle and pleasurable approach.

A deeper look into Mana Movement as a Feminine Healing Art…

The Art of Feminine Healing lays deep in the foundation of Holistic and Naturally aligned practices- ones that engage the whole being- in mind, body, and spirit reconnection. Aligning with the pulse of nature both within and without in order to foster emotional, spiritual, and physical well-being.

These Arts focus on the much-forgotten strengths we all contain, such as softness, intuition, and creativity, and provide a space of deep presence and intention to bring sacred ritual into the way we meet ourselves and the world- this is the Feminine Way!

Through Feminine Healing Arts such as Mana Movement, we are invited to embrace both our raw humanness in this flesh-and-bone vessel, as well as our divinity in the sacred breath and waves of emotion that flows through us. We realign with the natural Feminine Pathways and thus come into easeful coherence with every facet of the Universe (both within and without).

As is the nature of the Feminine, the Healing Arts are meant to meet us in ease and softness. To awaken a sense of both safety and freedom and a feel like a path to come back home- this is Mana Movement, a homecoming to your true self and own warm embrace.

Through Mana Movement, we find a path that fits us, that feels natural, and that invites us to return to the Art of Feminine Healing.

Hi, I’m Mana Mei. The Founder of Mana Movement

I began my lifelong devotion to movement through classical ballet training when I was 3 years old, and studied through University, where I also studied Modern Dance, Somatic Dance, Improvisation, and various other movement modalities. During University years I also discovered music festivals, and psychedelics, which opened up my mind and body to using dance in a different way, as a way to express raw emotion, connect with the unseen spiritual realms, and access a sense of freedom.

During my late twenties, I also discovered ecstatic dance, a space dedicated solely to free expression through movement. In my late twenties I also was going through a dark night of the soul as I was in a toxic relationship, and found myself powerless and stuck in patterns of unworthiness.

Ecstatic Dance became my therapy. Twice a week I would lay on the floor and move my body however it wanted, processing all the pain and intense emotions that had no other outlet.

Over the next 3 years, the intensity of my life continued and dance became my saving grace- it literally saved my life. I not only had an outlet and a safe space to be with myself and my emotions, but I gained tools for expressing and releasing pain and coming back home to myself.

I became absolutely certain that dance was an incredibly powerful modality of healing that was far underused and unknown. So I became passionate and curious to figure out how to make movement medicine available and accessible to more people.

I knew that my own success with using movement as medicine was because of my vast knowledge of somatics and dance technique as well as high level of kinesthetic awareness. So I began my journey of developing a method to help people learn to use dance as a healing modality through an accessible step-by-step practice… and so birthed MANA MOVEMENT!

I created Mana Movement to be a bridge. A pathway for ANYONE interested to use dance as a way to heal and transform, and it has been a huge success! Mana Movement has already supported THOUSANDS of women around the world along their healing journeys as an accessible daily practice for returning to wholeness.

Since creating Mana Movement nearly a decade ago, it has become the center pillar of my own personal self care regimen. Mana Movement has supported me through so many different moments of my journey- from moments of self doubt, fear, pain, grief, through pregnancy and birth and postpartum. Through finding balance between being a business owner, a mother, an artist, and a woman. Mana Movement has saved me from burnout and depression and I thank my lucky stars every day for the existence of this practice!

This is why I am so excited to share this incredible movement medicine with women just like me, who are navigating the ups and downs, ebb and flow of life in the eternal dance of balance.
