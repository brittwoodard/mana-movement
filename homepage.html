Mana Movement® is a holistic personal wellness practice combining somatic movement and embodied dance through a Holistic integration for personal healing and transformation.

This simple yet profound practice flows through a series of guided movement sequences, focusing on spinal mobility, whole body fluidity, release, breath-body connection, and introducing the Feminine Pathways of movement into the whole body.

Mana Movement® is the graceful merging of dance & meditation.

Through cultivating sustained focus and presence with the feeling body, Mana Movement® encourages harmonious reconnection to your physical and energy body. Through roots of somatic and embodied dance, Mana Movement® awakens creative life force energy and encourages authentic expression.

Mana Movement® focuses on moving “from the inside-out” - feeling over form - and opening the pathway to somatic healing through the holistic trifecta of mindfulness meditation, physical activation, and spiritual liberation.

The Mana Movement Practice is composed of simple, guided movement exercises, focused on the Feminine Paths of Movement- circles, spirals, and waves.

Returning to these innate movement patterns with the intention of softening and opening will awaken the natural flow of Life Force Energy through the body, supporting you to feel a deep sense of clarity, peace, expansion, and a renewed sense of inspiration.

Mana Movement is designed to suit all women, all bodies, at every stage of life and Feminine Rite of Passage.

The Full Mana Movement practice includes 4 primary body positions- seated, hands & knees, kneeling, and standing. Practices can range from 3 to 120 minutes and focus on a variety of intentions and purposes.

Mana Movement® incorporates conscious, intentional breathing patterns in relationship to each specific movement pattern in order to increase body connection, as well as increase the nervous system’s ability to self regulate through intentional activation and settling in a safe and held container of practice.
